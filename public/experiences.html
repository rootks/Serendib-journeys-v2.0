Highland Terroir

The Art of Pure Ceylon Tea

Join resident tea masters at dawn amidst the mist-clad hills of Nuwara Eliya and Dimbula. Learn why Ceylon tea remains the gold standard of world tea culture.

Don traditional bamboo baskets to pick the delicate "two leaves and a bud". Then journey into an operational colonial-era tea factory where you will witness withering, rolling, fermenting, and taste a flight of rare Silver Tips, Orange Pekoe, and BOPF with an expert sommelier.

Marine Ocean Safari

Sailing with Earth's Gentle Ocean Giants

Just six nautical miles off Mirissa, the underwater continental shelf plunges to depths exceeding 1,000 meters, forming a rich marine banquet for migrating Blue Whales.

Sail aboard our private eco-certified catamarans alongside trained marine biologists. Watch in awe as the largest creature to ever inhabit our planet breaches the ocean surface, raising its colossal fluke against the tropical sky.

Conservation-First Standards:

🐋 100m Ethical Distance Protocol 🎙️ Hydrophone Underwater Acoustics 🧑‍🔬 Resident Marine Biologist Guided ⛵ Stable Low-Emission Catamaran

Culinary Heritage

Clay-Pot Village Gastronomy

Sri Lankan cuisine is an intoxicating harmony of freshly roasted Ceylon cinnamon, roasted curry powders, and fresh coconut milk simmered over wood stoves in earthen pots.

Stroll through an organic spice garden to harvest wild curry leaves, bird's eye chilies, and lemongrass. Scrape fresh coconut using a traditional hand tool (hiramanaya), and slow-cook fragrant dhal, lagoon crab, and caramelized polos (baby jackfruit) curries served on fresh banana leaves.

Cooking Workshop Highlights:

🌶️ Whole Spice Blending & Roasting 🥥 Fresh Coconut Scraping Technique 🍳 Crispy Egg Hopper Making 🍌 Feast Served on Wild Banana Leaves

3,000-Year-Old Hela Wedakama & Ayurveda
